Zombie Rig Lab

The era 1 zombie cut into parts (head, torso, legs, forearms, hands and seven separate claws) and animated with code-driven bones. Pick a move, slow it down, switch on the skeleton to see the pivots. The panels on the right show the same zombie at real game size and how the game animates it today.

At game size

The rig drawn at the 54 px the zombie has in the game, doing the same move.

The game today

One painted picture moved as a whole, with a bob and a sway. Shown 1.6× larger than game size.

What it costs

One picture per enemy, cut into parts. Every move here is a few lines of code, so new moves (a lunge, a stagger, a special death) need no new art.

What to look for

Seams at the shoulders, hips and neck while the parts turn. These parts are cut straight from the existing sprite; a generated parts sheet with complete limbs would hide the joints even better.

Next step

If this level of motion is right, the same rig goes into the game for the zombie, and the other enemies follow with their own parts and moves.
