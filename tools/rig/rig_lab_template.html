What to judge

Does each enemy's walk and attack read as that creature at game size? The knight chops with his axe, the brute slams his gravestone, the crawler lashes its tail, the robot raises its rifle, the walker's pods kick back as they fire.

Known limits

Parts are cut from the finished picture, so big swings can show a hard edge at a joint. Machines with tracks (droid, tank) only rumble; their treads don't roll.
