What to check

  • Teeth sit inside the mouth, hanging from the lips
  • Two eyes, both fully in their sockets
  • Four fingers and a thumb per hand, claws on the tips
  • Shoulders, elbows and knees bend without tearing
  • Clothes stay on the body in every pose
  • Proportions: head, torso, arm and leg lengths
